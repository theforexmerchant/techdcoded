Privacy Policy

Once Upon Tingu Uploader · Operated by TechDcoded · Effective date: 30 September 2026

1. What this app is

Once Upon Tingu Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the "Once Upon Tingu" YouTube channel. It uploads videos, thumbnails and captions produced by the channel owner to the channel owner's own YouTube account using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information the app accesses

  • An OAuth access/refresh token for the channel owner's Google account, granted by the owner, used only to upload and manage videos, thumbnails and captions on the owner's own channel.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and upload status of videos the app itself uploaded), used only to confirm uploads succeeded.

The app does not access, collect or store any information about YouTube viewers, subscribers or commenters.

4. How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than uploading the owner's content.

5. Children's privacy

The "Once Upon Tingu" channel publishes content made for children and is set as "made for kids" on YouTube. This app does not collect any personal information from children or any other viewer. Viewing and interaction on YouTube are governed by YouTube's own policies.

6. Revoking access

Access granted to this app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from uploading. Locally stored tokens are deleted by the owner when the app is no longer used.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact: youtube@techdcoded.com

About TechDCoded

TechDCoded exists for one reason: most technology is explained badly. Manuals are dense, launch events are hype, and search results are written for advertisers. This site takes one piece of technology at a time and explains how it actually works, in plain language, with diagrams.

Who is behind TechDCoded

TechDCoded was founded by Bibekananda Patra (Founder, TechDCoded) and Omm Shree Dibya Dulabha Patra (Co-founder, TechDCoded).

Who writes this

I run TechDCoded, where I explain how everyday technology actually works — in short videos on YouTube and in written explainers here. Every article is researched from public sources and written to be understood without a technical background. You'll find the same explanations as one-minute videos on YouTube and across Instagram, Facebook and Telegram.

What we cover

  • Artificial intelligence and how the tools you use are built
  • Gadgets and consumer hardware
  • Science and innovation behind everyday devices
  • Fintech and digital payments
  • Future technology, explained without hype

How the articles are made

Every explainer is researched from public sources, written for a reader with no technical background, checked for accuracy and clarity, and published with the sources listed at the end. The full process is on our How we write page.

Contact

Corrections, questions and collaboration: business@techdcoded.com. If you spot an error in an article, tell us and we will fix it and note the update.
